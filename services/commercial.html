Commercial Roofing Systems

We install four primary commercial roofing systems, each engineered for specific building types, climate conditions, and performance requirements:

TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin)

Best for: New construction and re-roofing projects requiring energy efficiency

  • Energy Rating: ENERGY STAR certified, reflective white membrane
  • Installation: Heat-welded seams provide superior weather resistance
  • Durability: 20-30 year lifespan with proper maintenance
  • Cost: $5-8 per square foot installed
  • Advantages: Excellent UV resistance, lighter color reduces cooling costs, weldable seams eliminate adhesive risk
  • Maintenance: Regular inspections recommended annually

Popular choice for businesses prioritizing long-term energy savings and warranty protection.

EPDM (Rubber Roofing)

Best for: Buildings seeking proven, budget-friendly durability

  • Track Record: 50+ year proven performance history
  • Color Options: Available in black or white membranes
  • Durability: 25-30 year expected lifespan (often exceeds expectations)
  • Cost: $4-7 per square foot installed
  • Advantages: Most economical choice, excellent flexibility in temperature extremes, simple repairs
  • Note: Black EPDM absorbs more heat (lower energy efficiency)

Ideal for businesses seeking maximum value and proven longevity over decorative appeal.

Modified Bitumen

Best for: Buildings with active roof traffic and complex penetrations

  • Installation Methods: Torch-applied or cold-applied options available
  • Multi-Layer Design: Built-up surface provides exceptional wear protection
  • Durability: 20-25 year typical lifespan
  • Cost: $4-8 per square foot installed
  • Advantages: Excellent for high-traffic areas, accommodates numerous penetrations, proven in commercial applications
  • Maintenance: Periodic sealing recommended

Traditional choice for facilities with significant roof-level activity and equipment.

Commercial Metal Roofing

Best for: Premium projects requiring maximum longevity and aesthetic appeal

  • Styles Available: Standing seam, corrugated, R-panel profiles
  • Exceptional Durability: 40-60+ year lifespan (among the longest in the industry)
  • Cost: $8-15 per square foot installed
  • Advantages: Lightweight, excellent snow shedding, minimal maintenance, 100% recyclable, superior weather performance
  • Energy Options: Reflective coatings available for enhanced efficiency
  • Investment: Higher initial cost offset by decades of service

Premium choice for buildings designed for multi-generational ownership and operations.

Commercial Solar Solutions

Note: Commercial Solar Only. This section addresses commercial solar installations exclusively. Connecticut residential solar incentives are insufficient to warrant promotion; however, commercial installations benefit from robust federal programs and state incentives.

Why Commercial Solar Makes Financial Sense

Commercial solar installations operate under a fundamentally different economic model than residential systems. Businesses benefit from:

  • Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC): 30% tax credit on system costs through 2032
  • MACRS Depreciation: Accelerated 5-year depreciation schedule for tax purposes
  • Connecticut Commercial Incentives: Rebates and performance-based incentive programs
  • Operating Expense Reduction: Direct reduction in energy costs, improving profitability
  • Revenue Stability: Protection against rising electricity rates
  • Enhanced Property Value: Buildings with solar command premium valuations
  • Corporate Sustainability Goals: Demonstrates environmental commitment to clients and investors

Commercial Solar Installation Process

A successful commercial solar project requires careful assessment and planning:

1. Roof Assessment

Before solar installation, we conduct comprehensive roof evaluation including:

  • Structural load capacity analysis
  • Remaining roof system lifespan assessment
  • Roof orientation and shading evaluation
  • Weather pattern analysis for your location

If your roof requires replacement, we recommend upgrading the roofing system first—many businesses combine roof replacement with solar installation to maximize efficiency and minimize disruption.

2. System Design

Custom design based on:

  • Your historical electricity consumption patterns
  • Available roof square footage and orientation
  • Local Connecticut and NY solar irradiance data
  • Financial goals (maximum payback, net-zero, specific ROI target)

3. Financing & Incentives

We guide you through:

  • Federal ITC (30%) and state incentive applications
  • Commercial financing options (loans, leases, PPAs)
  • Tax strategy coordination with your accountant
  • Net metering agreements with your utility provider

4. Installation & Monitoring

Professional installation with:

  • Proper roof flashing and waterproofing integration
  • Real-time monitoring system for performance tracking
  • Comprehensive warranty coverage (system and installation)
  • Ongoing maintenance and performance optimization

Commercial Solar ROI Factors

Return on investment depends on multiple variables:

  • Electricity Costs: Higher baseline electricity rates = faster payback (CT and NY have above-average rates)
  • System Size: Larger systems generate greater annual savings
  • Roof Orientation: South-facing roofs outperform east/west (which still perform well)
  • Local Irradiance: Solar resource availability in your specific location
  • Federal & State Incentives: Can reduce effective payback period by 30-50%
  • Tax Situation: Businesses with sufficient tax liability maximize ITC and depreciation benefits
  • Financing Method: Owned systems typically outperform leases long-term (though leases offer lower upfront cost)

Commercial Roofing & Solar FAQ

How do I know if commercial solar is right for my business?

Commercial solar is typically viable if your business:

  • Has significant monthly electricity costs (typically $2,000+ monthly)
  • Occupies the property long-term (or has sufficient roof remaining lease term)
  • Operates during daylight hours when solar generation occurs
  • Has sufficient roof space with favorable orientation (south, east, or west-facing)

Even if one factor seems limiting, modern solar design can often optimize installations. A free feasibility assessment determines viability for your specific situation.

What's the difference between commercial and residential solar incentives?

Connecticut residential solar incentives are limited and often insufficient to support attractive financial returns. However, commercial installations benefit from:

  • Federal ITC: 30% of system cost (available through 2032)
  • MACRS Depreciation: 5-year accelerated depreciation for tax purposes
  • Connecticut Commercial Programs: State-level incentives and rebates for business installations
  • Operational Tax Deductions: Maintenance and energy storage costs

This fundamentally different economic structure makes commercial solar projects substantially more attractive financially than residential systems in Connecticut.

Can I install solar on an aging roof, or should I replace the roof first?

We recommend roof assessment before solar planning. Generally:

  • If roof has 15+ years remaining life: Solar can be installed on current roof
  • If roof is approaching end of life (10-15 years): Consider combining roof replacement with solar installation
  • If roof requires imminent replacement: Replace roof first, then install solar on new system

Installing solar on an aging roof wastes your investment when the roof inevitably requires replacement—the solar system would need to be removed and reinstalled. Combining both projects optimizes long-term cost and efficiency.

What warranty coverage do commercial roofing and solar systems include?

Our installations include comprehensive warranties:

  • Roofing Systems: Material warranties (15-25 years typically) + workmanship warranty (10+ years). Premium metal roofs include extended coverage up to 50 years.
  • Solar Panels: 25-30 year performance warranties (output guarantee) + 10+ year equipment warranty
  • Inverters: 10-15 year coverage on power conversion equipment
  • Installation Labor: Comprehensive workmanship warranty protecting your investment

All warranties are transferable if your business changes ownership, adding value for future sale.
