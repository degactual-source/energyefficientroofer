Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

How long does a roof replacement typically take?

Most roof replacements take 2-5 days depending on size, complexity, and weather. We work efficiently while maintaining our high-quality standards. We'll give you a specific timeline during your inspection.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We're currently managing $1M+ in active insurance claims. We handle the entire process for you—submitting documentation, negotiating with adjusters, and often getting you approved at zero out-of-pocket cost.

What areas do you service?

We primarily serve Connecticut, plus Westchester County, Rockland County, and Putnam County in New York. If you're in a neighboring area, call us—we may be able to help.

What warranty do you offer?

We stand behind our work with industry-leading warranties. Details vary by service and materials. Your custom proposal will include complete warranty information.

Do you offer residential solar installation?

We specialize in commercial solar only. For residential solar, we have trusted partners we can refer you to. Call us for recommendations.

How much does a roof inspection cost?

Our comprehensive roof inspection is completely free. No hidden charges, no obligation. We'll give you an honest assessment and detailed report of any work needed.
