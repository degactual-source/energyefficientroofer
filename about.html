Dom's Story

I grew up watching my father—a mechanic—fix things the right way. Not the fast way. Not the cheap way. The right way. He taught me that shortcuts cost homeowners money down the road, and that a job done well the first time is the only job worth doing.

My stepdad was a builder, so I learned construction from someone who understood that exterior work is what stands between families and harsh New England weather. I learned early that there's no room for shortcuts on a roof or in a gutter system.

"I've been on roofs. I understand the work. I know what corners look like when they're cut, and I refuse to cut them."

For the past several years, I've built an $8M operation through George's Seamless Gutters, completing 1,200+ jobs annually. We've earned the trust of Connecticut homeowners and contractors through consistency, quality, and showing up when others don't.

Now, I'm expanding that mission through Energy Efficient Roofer. I'm not some corporate suit in an office. I've been in the trenches. I understand the pressure contractors face to cut corners, and I've chosen a different path. I also understand the pressure homeowners face when insurance companies shortchange claims, and I've built processes to protect them.

This company exists because I genuinely give a damn about doing things right—and because homeowners deserve someone who does too.

Our Mission

Connecticut homeowners face three threats:

  • The harsh New England weather that batters roofs, gutters, and exterior systems year after year.
  • Contractors who cut corners to maximize profit—leaving you with leaks, failures, and stress.
  • Insurance companies that shortchange claims—leaving homeowners to pay out of pocket or live with incomplete repairs.

Energy Efficient Roofer exists to address all three. We protect your home from weather. We do the work right the first time, so you don't face surprise failures. And we partner with you during insurance claims to ensure you get what you're entitled to.

Our mission is simple: be the contractor you actually trust.

The Team & Values

We operate on a few core principles that guide everything we do:

  • Quality Over Speed — We don't rush jobs. We don't cut corners. We don't chase volume at the expense of excellence. If it takes longer to do it right, we do it the right way.
  • Education Over Sales Pressure — We explain what we're doing and why. You should understand your roof, your gutters, your options, and the trade-offs. No pressure. No upselling. Just honest conversation.
  • Transparency in Pricing — No surprises. No hidden fees. You know what you're paying and why before we start work.
  • Documentation of Everything — We use CompanyCam to photograph and document every step of every project. You can see what we're doing, when we're doing it, and the quality we deliver. That documentation also protects you during insurance claims.
  • Stand Behind the Work — Our reputation is built on the quality of our work. We warranty what we do because we know it's done right.
