What happens during a free roof inspection with EER?

Mike shows up at the scheduled time — not a four-hour arrival window, not a subcontractor you've never met. He walks the roof, examines the condition of the shingles, flashing, ridge, penetrations, gutters, and ventilation. For storm damage inspections, he documents everything with photos before any work begins.

After the inspection, Mike gives you a straight answer about what he found. If there's no damage that warrants a claim or repair, he'll tell you. EER doesn't manufacture work where there isn't any. That reputation for honesty is a deliberate business decision — referrals come from homeowners who trusted the assessment they got, not from homeowners who felt sold.

If there is damage, Mike walks you through what he found, what the repair or replacement scope looks like, and — if you have insurance coverage that may apply — how the claims process works. He can manage the entire insurance process from documentation through final check if that's needed.

The inspection is free. There's no charge and no obligation to hire EER for the work. The goal of the inspection is to give you accurate information. What you do with it is your call.
